I’m Sungjae, a KAIST graduate with a double major in Physics and Computer Science. I study how machine learning models represent information and how those representations shape what a model can learn.

My work spans one-step image generation and spatial transcriptomics—from exploring feature encoders for generative models to learning about cells in their tissue context.

Selected research

Questions, methods & projects

GENERATIVE MODELING

I-Drift

One-step image generation

Exploring how feature encoders and training objectives influence image generation in a single forward pass, drawing on Drifting Models, Priming Models, diffusion, and flow matching.

Research focus

I compare the information captured by different pretrained encoders and the learning signals they provide to a generator, with attention to both generation quality and training cost.

SPATIAL REPRESENTATION LEARNING

Bio-Architectural Graph Modeling

BAGM · Spatial transcriptomics

Learning cell representations from gene expression and spatial neighborhoods in gastric tissue. Geometry-aware graph attention and masked-expression prediction connect cellular information with tissue organization.

Paper (PDF) Code & project details
Research focus

I investigate what spatial context adds beyond a cell’s own features, and how learned representations can support tissue analysis and the study of candidate gene and cell dependencies.
