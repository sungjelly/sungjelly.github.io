I’m SungJae, a KAIST graduate with a double major in Physics and Computer Science. I’m interested in how AI models learn representations, generate new data, and generalize.

My work has explored spatial biology through BAGM and one-step image generation through I-Drift. I approach new areas of AI through focused experiments, with a common interest in how architectures and learning objectives shape model behavior.
